# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

General audience making day-to-day decisions. They arrive with a specific need (calculate a car loan, check EV charging costs, figure out a date) and want to complete their task quickly without learning an interface. They range from tech-comfortable to casual phone users. They're not developers, not power users. They're someone sitting at a desk or on a phone who needs an answer and then wants to leave.

## Product Purpose

A collection of focused, single-purpose tools that solve everyday calculation and reference problems instantly. Success is measured by speed to value: a visitor arrives, gets their answer, and leaves. No accounts, no onboarding friction, no feature bloat. Each tool does one thing well.

The homepage is a brand surface that introduces these tools and establishes trust and personality. The tools themselves are product surfaces that must deliver excellent usability for a broad public audience.

## Brand Personality

Casual, inclusive, professional. Feels like a well-designed coffee shop menu: approachable, clear, no pretension. Not playful or whimsical, not cold or corporate. Light on effort, serious on craft.

Three-word personality: casual, inclusive, professional.

Emotional goals: comfort, clarity, confidence. The visitor should feel like they're in capable hands without being talked down to.

## Anti-references

- **skillsmp.com**: Dark mode by default, buttons indistinguishable from text, clickable elements lack affordance. Avoid: ambiguous interactivity, dark-for-dark's-sake, flat hierarchy between actions and content.

## Design Principles

1. **Arrive, solve, leave.** Every tool should be usable within 3 seconds of landing. No learning curve, no modes, no setup.
2. **Obvious interactivity.** If something can be clicked, tapped, or interacted with, it should look like it. No mystery-meat navigation.
3. **One thing per page.** Each tool has one job. Resist scope creep. The best tool is the one that does less but does it perfectly.
4. **Casual confidence.** Professional doesn't mean stiff. The interface should feel like it was made by someone competent who doesn't need to prove it with decoration.
5. **Light touch.** Warm neutrals, breathable spacing, nothing shouting. The design serves the task, not the other way around.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA as a baseline
- Broad audience means-mobile-first: large touch targets (44px minimum), clear contrast on light backgrounds
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion
- Plain, jargon-free language in all UI copy
